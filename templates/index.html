Expected columns: Partner Code, Partner Name, Total, GST No, GST, Address, PAN, Bank Name, Bank Account Number, ifsc code, Email. (Address, PAN and Email are optional — Email is only needed if you plan to send invoices out from the Email Config tab.)
Pick the payout month — it will appear on invoices as e.g. "Apr-2023".
Pick the invoice date — it will appear on invoices as e.g. "30-Apr-2023".
GST logic applied automatically:
No GST No on file → no tax lines, invoice = Total as-is.
GST No present, not starting with 33 → the sheet's GST value is charged in full as IGST.
GST No present, starting with 33 → the sheet's GST value is split in half between SGST and CGST.
(Total is the base commission amount; the tax amount comes straight from the GST column and is added on top: grand total = Total + GST.)
